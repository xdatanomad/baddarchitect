# Social Workflow

This file covers **on-demand** social derivatives created from a published
article. For the **scheduled, semi-automated** LinkedIn pipeline (idea proposed
from recent project work, reviewed in a GitHub issue, published through a gate),
see `docs/operations/linkedin-post-workflow.md`. Its worklog lives in
`content/social/worklog/`.

## Folders

- `content/social/linkedin-plans/` - article-specific LinkedIn promotion plans.
- `content/social/linkedin-short-posts/` - short LinkedIn posts and short video scripts.
- `content/social/linkedin-long-posts/` - longer LinkedIn article summaries or essays.
- `content/social/worklog/` - log of the automated LinkedIn post pipeline (ideas, parked, scheduled, published, dropped).

## Rules

- Tie each social asset to a source essay, field note, or guide.
- Preserve the direct, candid, practical project tone.
- Do not turn nuanced articles into hype-heavy claims.
- Prefer practical hooks, specific examples, and clear reader value.
